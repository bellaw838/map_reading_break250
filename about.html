About

I'm a student in SAS who has experienced problems with MAP Reading throughout the years. Turns out, it wasn't only me who had this problem. A lot of people in my grade did too.

Thus, I decided to consult AI, and AI told me something I found useful: beyond vocabulary, breaking 250 is not about reading speed or reading more books — it is about vocabulary nuance, finding real evidence, careful inference, and noticing an author's tone.

This site is made to be simple and helpful from the beginning: short mini-lessons, and quizzes where every wrong answer explains what you got wrong. It's expected to take you around 5 hours in total. AI also helped mark up public-domain texts the way a strong reader marks them. Initially, this site was made to help me, but I am hoping that it can help many others as well.

Thanks to AI advancements — models became much smarter in 2026. Without writing any code, I built the first version of this site in a few days. An Apple app is planned next.
