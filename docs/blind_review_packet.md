# Blind Review Packet — Break 250 Reading

**67 questions.** Answer each one WITHOUT seeing the answer key.

For every question, please give:

1. **Your answer** (A/B/C/D).
2. **Your textual reason** — the exact words that make it best supported.
3. **Second-best choice**, and why it loses.
4. **Any question where you think two answers are equally defensible.**
5. **Any feedback-free concerns**: unclear wording, an unfair distractor,
   a claim stronger than the passage proves, a narrator/speaker/author
   mix-up, or a rule that depends on one word alone.

Answer in a numbered list. Do not skip items.
