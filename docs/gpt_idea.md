# Product Idea: Thinking Habit Trainer

## Core Positioning

This product does not need to be a full reading course or a complete MAP simulator.

For strong readers already around RIT 230-240+, the highest-value use case is a short, focused **"how to think while reading"** reset. These students usually already read at school, at home, and in other programs such as Achieve3000. The missing piece is often not more reading volume. The missing piece is using advanced reading-analysis moves automatically while reading unfamiliar text.

In that sense, this app can be valuable as a 3-5 hour reference starter:

> Learn the reading moves that separate strong readers from 250+ test readers.

## Why This Matters For Breaking MAP 250

Most of the concepts are probably already taught in school:

- Tone
- Theme
- Main idea / central idea
- Evidence
- Inference
- Word choice
- Structure
- Author's purpose
- Paragraph function

But being taught a term is different from using it while reading.

Many strong readers read as readers: they follow the plot, understand the article, and remember the main points. MAP 250-level questions often require a different habit:

> Do not just read what the text says. Notice what the author is doing and what the text proves.

That habit is likely one of the key levers for moving from strong reading to 250+ performance.

## The Real Gap

The problem is usually not:

> The student has never heard of theme or inference.

The problem is:

> Can the student automatically use the right reading move without being told which move to use?

A strong reader may understand a passage and still miss a high-level question because they:

- Read for plot, not author choices.
- Know the main idea, but cannot choose the best evidence.
- Feel the tone, but cannot identify the word-choice signal.
- Infer too far beyond the text.
- Choose a true answer instead of the best-supported answer.
- Miss a paragraph's function or a structural shift.

## Product Scope

The mandatory experience should stay fast:

- The 3 MAP-aligned sections.
- Short concept explanations.
- Fast practice.
- Short passages/examples that show the thinking moves.

The optional experience can be deeper:

- Reading Lab
- Library
- Passage-anchored practice sets
- Readiness checks

Passage-anchored scored practice is important, but it does not need to be mandatory for the first experience. Making everything test-like too early may scare away the exact students this product is meant to help: strong, busy readers who need a compact thinking reset.

## Honest Promise

Good promise:

> In 3-5 hours, this app helps you learn the reading-thinking moves that matter most for advanced MAP Reading questions.

Avoid promising:

> Complete this and you will break 250.

Better framing:

> This is a thinking habit trainer, not a full reading curriculum and not an official MAP score predictor.

## Habit To Teach

The core habit is simple:

> Pause occasionally and ask: What is the author doing here? What proves it? What changed? What is implied but not directly said?

Students do not need to mark every sentence forever. But practicing this habit every now and then in real reading is valuable because it trains the move MAP 250+ often rewards: reading with evidence, inference, tone, structure, and purpose in mind.
