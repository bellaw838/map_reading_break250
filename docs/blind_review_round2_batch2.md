# Blind Review — Round 2, Batch 2 of 4

**46 questions.**

You are an experienced middle-school English teacher reviewing
multiple-choice reading questions for advanced readers (ages 10-14, MAP Reading
240-250).

Answer every question **without any answer key**. I am checking whether each
question has one clearly best answer.

For each question give:

1. Your answer (A/B/C/D).
2. The exact words that make it best supported.
3. Your second-best choice, and why it loses.
4. A verdict: **Clear**, **Revise**, or **Ambiguous/invalid**.

Then flag separately any item where:

- two answers are equally defensible;
- the wording is unclear or a distractor is unfair;
- the intended answer claims more than the passage proves;
- narrator, speaker and author are confused;
- the answer depends on one word rather than context.

Do not be generous. If an item is ambiguous, say so.

Start your reply with a compact answer sequence in this exact format so it can
be scored automatically:

**1-10:** A, B, C, ...
**11-20:** ...

---
