# About

I am a student from SAS Pudong. In May 2026 I scored under 250 in MAP Reading again. I had been stuck in the 240s for a year, and reading a lot more books was not moving the score.

My dad suggested I should consult AI about it. AI told me something I found useful: beyond vocabulary, breaking 250 is not about reading speed or reading more stories — it is about vocabulary nuance, finding real evidence, careful inference, and noticing an author's tone. I could not find a resource dedicated just to these skills (NoRedInk is more about grammar, and Achieve3000 is more general reading practice), so with AI's suggestion I decided to build one, the same way I built my word choice site.

This site is made to be simple and helpful from the beginning: short mini-lessons, and quizzes where every wrong answer explains the trap you fell into. It's expected to take you around 5 hours in total. AI also helped mark up public-domain texts the way a strong reader marks them. I made the website for my own use, but I hope it can help other students stuck at the same plateau.

*Thanks to AI advancements — models became much smarter in 2026. Without writing any code, I built the first version of this site in a few days. An Apple app is planned next.*
